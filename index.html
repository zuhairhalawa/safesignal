How SafeSignal reached this & its limits

    SafeSignal is a learning aid, not a guarantee. It explains common scam hallmarks in text you paste; it cannot verify a sender, open links, or catch every scam. When in doubt, contact the organisation through a channel you already trust. In an emergency, contact local authorities — this is not a replacement for emergency services.
