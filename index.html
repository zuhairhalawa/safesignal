How it decided, and what it can't do

    SafeSignal is a learning aid, not a guarantee. It reads the text you paste and explains common scam signs — it can't verify a sender, open links, or catch every scam. When unsure, reach the company through a number or app you already trust. In an emergency, contact your local authorities.
